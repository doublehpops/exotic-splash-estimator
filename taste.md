# Estimator taste

Stay with the V7 staff shell. Do not add a second palette or a separate mock page.

- Paper background `#f5f5f0`, ink `#192e2b`, green `#163d37`, lime `#dceb9c`, warning wash `#fff0ec`.
- Type: Manrope for headings, DM Sans for body. Existing sizes only.
- Owner-only warnings use `.warn-banner`. Informational locks use `.info-banner`.
- Money tables keep the current `th` / `td.num` pattern.
- Customer estimates stay on the quieter `estimate.html` card. Do not put internal labels, proposal provenance, or cost columns there.
- Copy for employees names “approved selling price” and “not on this account.” Do not explain pay, margin, or markup on employee or customer screens.
